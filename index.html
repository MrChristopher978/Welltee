§ 05 — METHOD
01. Rule One

No bloat.

One product, one job. Every feature earns its place. The thing you came to do is one tap away. We'd rather ship a tool that does one thing right than a platform that does ten things badly.

RULE-01 · DISCIPLINE ↓ KEEP READING
02. Rule Two

No ads.

You are the customer, not the inventory. We don't sell your attention to anyone, ever. Software isn't a billboard, it's a tool — and a tool you rent every month is a tool that owns you.

RULE-02 · RESPECT ↓ KEEP READING
03. Rule Three

No subscriptions you can't escape.

Cancel from inside the app, in two taps. Export your data, take it with you, walk away when you want. Just software that respects your time and the work you do.
